**EN**

## Definition / 定义

**EN**
Symbioship is the governance relation for cooperation among autonomous beings: participants Registered (who collaborates), evidence Verified (collaborative output testable), and changes Gated (controlled). In practice: separation of four powers — proposal, execution, verification, memory.

**ZH**
共生协作治理是多自治之物协作时的治理关系态：注册（谁在协作）、证据（协作产出可验证）、门禁（协作变更受控）。实践：四权分离（提议/执行/验证/记忆）。

## Verification / 查重与验证

**EN**
Prior-art search v2: zero conflicts. Practice: four-power separation in research collectives.

**ZH**
查重报告 v2（全 0）。实践：理论研究团四权分离。
